# Pay a member enters is private, and counts as known pay for that member

Many postings state no pay, and the estimate (ADR 0002) is only a guess. Members often learn the real figure, from a recruiter or a conversation, or can tell the estimate is right. We decided a member may record pay for a job, by typing it in or confirming the estimate, and that it is theirs alone.

## Consequences

- Recorded pay belongs to the member, like the rest of their data (rule 9). Other members still see the posting's pay or the estimate. One member's mistake can't change anyone else's matches.
- For that member, recorded pay counts as known pay: their firm pay limits check it like stated pay, and target fit reads it. It replaces a posting's stated pay for them too, since a member who records pay knows something newer.
- It is always labelled as pay they entered or an estimate they confirmed, never shown as stated by the posting.
- Recording pay re-checks that member's firm limits for the job straight away, because no model is involved. Scores change only when the member rescores (rule 5).
- A confirmed estimate is a copy. If the estimate later changes, the member's figure stays until they change it.
