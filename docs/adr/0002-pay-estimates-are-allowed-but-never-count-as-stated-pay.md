# Pay estimates are allowed but never count as stated pay

The app's central rule is that it does not invent facts, and missing pay is shown as unknown. Many postings omit pay, though, and an unknown is of little use when deciding where to spend effort, so we decided the app may estimate a pay range for a job that states none, as a deliberate and bounded exception.

## Consequences

- An estimate always carries a certainty (low, medium or high) and is always labelled as an estimate wherever it appears.
- An estimate is never checked against a firm limit. A firm pay limit sees the job's pay as unknown and follows the user's setting for unknowns, so a guess can never hide a job.
- An estimate may lower or raise target fit, and the reason must say that it rests on an estimate.
- An estimate is made once per job and shared across users.
- The exception covers job pay only. Nothing about a user is ever estimated: resumes and cover letters still draw only on the master profile.
