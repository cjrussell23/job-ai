Hours of Work: 36.25 hours per week
Permanent/Temporary/Wage: Permanent

The Alberta Public Service works to build a stronger province for current and future generations. We make a difference in the lives of Albertans through rewarding career opportunities. For more information, please visit: https://www.alberta.ca/about-the-alberta-public-service

 

Technology and Innovation is responsible for making innovation and technology the driving force behind Alberta’s economic growth and diversification. To learn more about us, please visit: https://www.alberta.ca/technology-and-innovation.

 

The Digital Design and Delivery (DDD) division is the Government of Alberta’s centre of digital delivery. It partners with ministries to design simpler, faster, and better services; build capability and confidence in modern digital practices; and assure service quality and value through standards and controls. Multidisciplinary product teams are assigned priority GoA projects and employ an agile, product-oriented approach in providing solutions for ministry partners.

 

Within DDD, the Software Delivery Branch provides architectural leadership, CloudOps, DevOps practices, AI engineering, and software delivery excellence across product and platform teams. We shape how digital solutions are designed, evolved, and governed, balancing innovation with reliability, security, and public sector responsibility.

Role Responsibilities

Reporting to the Director, AI Technical Renewal, the Manager, AI Harness Engineering delivers expertise in technical analysis, design, implementation, support and operations of IMT services. This position will work with and provide direction to a combination GoA staff, contractors and external service providers.

 

The Manager is accountable for the design and delivery of AI Software Harnesses leverage across the GoA; provides oversight and guidance to the Harness Engineering team; works with other IT and business teams; responsible for implementation and continuous delivery of AI Software Sustainment.

 

The responsibilities of this role include:

  • Managing AI software sustainment platforms to ensure service reliability, availability, and continuous improvement.
  • Providing technical leadership on AI-enabled solutions, automation, system integrations, and emerging technologies.
  • Leading complex projects and coordinating priorities, resources, testing, remediation, and delivery across stakeholders.
  • Establishing technical standards, procedures, controls, and documentation aligned with security, audit, and GoA requirements.
  • Overseeing incident, problem, change, service, and capacity management practices to support effective service delivery.
  • Leading, mentoring, and developing technical staff while assigning priorities and monitoring progress.

 

To be successful in this role, you will demonstrate:

  • Strong technical leadership in AI-enabled solutions, automation, and emerging technologies.
  • Strategic thinking and sound judgment in addressing complex technical and operational challenges.
  • Effective communication and collaboration with technical teams, business partners, and senior stakeholders.
  • Proven ability to lead, mentor, and develop high-performing technical teams.


The Job Description is provided for further details on this position. 

APS Competencies

Competencies are behaviors that are essential to reach our goals in serving Albertans. We encourage you to have an in depth understanding of the competencies that are required for this opportunity and to be prepared to demonstrate them during the recruitment process.

Qualifications

Requirements:

  • A university degree in a related field (e.g., Computer Science, Information Technology), supplemented by four (4) years of progressively responsible related experience.
  • Experience with designing AI Harnesses for automation of complex processes, like end-to-end software delivery.
  • Experience with AI-led delivery approaches.
  • Experience integrating, using, or developing AI-enabled services.
  • A cover letter clearly demonstrating relevant skills and competencies tailored to this position.

 

Equivalencies:

  • A related master’s degree and a minimum of three (3) years of related experience; or
  • A related two-year diploma and a minimum of six (6) years of related experience; or
  • A related one-year certificate and a minimum of seven (7) years of related experience; or
  • A minimum of eight (8) years of related experience in lieu of formal education.

 

Assets:

  • AI Academy training.
  • Experience delivering enterprise solutions.
