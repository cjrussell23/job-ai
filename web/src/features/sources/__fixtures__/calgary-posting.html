As a Partnership Specialist, you will advance the social well-being of Calgarians by managing The City's partnerships with non-profit organizations that deliver community well-being social service programs. Primary duties include: 
  • Oversee contracts with non-profit organizations delivering community well-being social programs and services to ensure that requirements and outcomes are being met. 
  • Monitor the effectiveness of funded programs and the organizations delivering them, using performance measurement systems and other evaluation tools. 
  • Inform the community well-being social services funding recommendation and decision-making process. 
  • Manage and resolve issues as they arise and escalate as necessary. 
  • Inform corporate and department accountability reporting. 
  • Prepare and present written reports, briefing notes, memos and presentations, as required. 
  • Participate in or lead project-based work. 
Qualifications 
  • A degree in a related field plus at least 5 years of relevant experience in the social services field, with emphasis on partner relationships, non-profit management, risk assessment, governance and partnership development.
  • A valid Class 5 Driver's License (or provincial equivalency) and access to a personal vehicle for business use is required.
  • Experience with Family and Community Support Services (FCSS) outcome tracking system or other performance measurement systems will be considered an asset.
  • Previous experience and knowledge of non-profit organizations and those who deliver social services programs is also an asset.
